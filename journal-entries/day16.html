Journal Entry - Day 16

Date: October 21, 2024

Reflections:

I feel that my sleep is becoming lighter, almost as if I was on a retreat. I can see my mind wander in imaginations for most part of the night, so that I enter and leave deep sleep aware of its movements and can feel it throughout the night when I’m half sleep half awake. There is no rest for the mind, it plays the same games than when it’s awake, even more indulgently. I will go to sleep past midnight, as I have been talking with some people for quite a few hours. I cannot say that I broke any training precept, yet I feel a little bit agitated from the talk. I did not lie, gossip, or use harsh words, but I did joke with a possibly inappropriate topic. It made the people laugh quite a bit, which is wholesome, but I’m not sure weather overall it was skilful talk. We also ate together, and I was snacking, without noticing weather I was hungry or not and purely out of fun and pleasure. So, I would say that the training rule regarding restraint in food – that is, eating in one session – was broken. As for the rest I did follow them. But these training rules are very gross. Every single moment the hindrances attack and at so many moments I can fall into them without needing to say: “I’m breaking a training precept.” There will never be enough precepts to guide oneself to the overcoming of the hindrances, they can only create favourable conditions so that it’s easier for the mind to adopt the sufficient awareness and skill. Virtue is the task of the rules, for they regard sila, but as far as the overcoming of the hindrances, only samadhi can take me there.
