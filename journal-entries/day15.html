Journal Entry - Day 15

Date: October 20, 2024

Reflections:

I have not been calm enough to feel sad. Instead, I have most prominently felt restless. That is why in the afternoon I decided to go for a walk in nature. I had not explored the surroundings and was surprised at how quickly I could enter a thick forest. I got lost in it for a bit over an hour as the sun was setting and arrived home when it was already dark. It was healing, I felt much calmer, but of course it’s not an end solution. I have followed all training precepts.
